Scholarships for Bangladeshi Students

This page collects verified scholarship opportunities that are open to Bangladeshi students and recent graduates. This collection currently contains 35 matching opportunities. Each listing includes funding details, degree level, deadline, host country, and a direct link to the official provider. These scholarships are sourced from governments, universities, foundations, and international organizations that accept applicants from Bangladeshi backgrounds.

35 matching listings.

What types of scholarships are included

The listings on this page cover the range of funding models — fully funded awards covering tuition and living costs, partially funded awards, and targeted awards for specific fields or career stages. Bangladeshi applicants can compare programs by funding level, destination country, degree level (undergraduate, master's, PhD), and field of study. Each listing is verified before publication for deadline clarity, eligibility rules, and funding information.

Application process for international scholarships

Most of these programs require: verified academic transcripts, a motivation letter or personal statement, reference letters, and language proficiency evidence where applicable. Some require research proposals, portfolios, or institutional nomination. Bangladeshi applicants should plan for document certification, translation where needed, and visa timelines. Start applications 8-12 weeks before each deadline to allow time for referee coordination and document preparation.

Tips for Bangladeshi applicants

Focus on programs that match your academic level and field. Compare funding packages carefully — some cover full costs while others require supplementary funding. Keep a checklist of deadlines, required documents, and application portals. Apply through the official provider channels only. Review the eligibility criteria for each program thoroughly before preparing your application package.

Frequently asked questions

How many scholarships for bangladeshi students are currently listed?

How do I know which scholarships for bangladeshi students to prioritize?

Compare listings by deadline proximity, funding level, and how well they match your academic background or career goals. Starting with programs whose deadlines are furthest out gives you more preparation time.

Are these scholarships open to all Bangladeshi students?

Each program sets its own eligibility rules. Some are open to all Bangladeshi nationals, while others target specific regions, age groups, or academic levels within Bangladeshi. Check the eligibility section of each listing before applying.

Do Bangladeshi students need an offer of admission before applying?

Some scholarships require a separate admission application to the university first, while others bundle admission and funding in one process. Read the application instructions for each program to understand the sequence.
