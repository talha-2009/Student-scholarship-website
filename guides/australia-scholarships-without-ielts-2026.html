What is an MOI Letter?

A Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter is a certificate issued by your previous university confirming that your degree program was taught entirely in English. This is the most cost-effective alternative to IELTS. Most Australian universities accept MOI letters from institutions in countries where English is the primary language or from programs officially taught in English.

Which Tests Are Accepted for Australian Student Visas?

The Australian Department of Home Affairs accepts IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, and Cambridge English (C1 Advanced) for student visa applications. Duolingo is NOT accepted for visa purposes, though some universities accept it for admission only.

Required Documents for Australia Scholarship Applications 2026–2027

Standard documents required for most Australian scholarship applications:

  • Passport — Valid passport copy (bio-data page)
  • Academic Transcripts — Certified copies of all previous degrees with grade translation if needed
  • CV/Resume — Academic CV highlighting research, publications, work experience, and awards
  • Recommendation Letters — 2–3 letters from professors or employers on official letterhead
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP) — 500–1,000 words explaining your academic goals and why you chose the program
  • Research Proposal — Required for PhD and research master's applications (2,000–4,000 words)
  • English Proficiency Proof — MOI letter, PTE, TOEFL, Cambridge, or Duolingo score report
  • Medium of Instruction (MOI) Letter — From your previous university confirming English-medium instruction

Tips for Strong Documents

  • Request your MOI letter at least 2 weeks before the deadline
  • Tailor each SOP to the specific university and program
  • Get recommendation letters from academic referees who know your work well
  • For research programs, contact a potential supervisor before applying — their support significantly increases your chances
  • Use a clear, professional email address and format all documents as PDF

Application Process — Step by Step for 2026–2027 Intake

  1. Identify eligible scholarships — Review the comparison table above and shortlist 3–5 programs that match your profile, degree level, and preferred intake.
  2. Check English requirements — Verify whether the scholarship accepts MOI (free), PTE, TOEFL, or another alternative. Prepare your chosen alternative early.
  3. Prepare documents — Gather all required documents. Request MOI letter from your previous institution. Get transcripts attested if needed.
  4. Contact potential supervisors — For research degrees (Master's by Research, PhD), email professors whose research aligns with yours. Attach a brief CV and research proposal.
  5. Write your SOP/research proposal — Craft a compelling statement explaining your academic journey, research interests, career goals, and why you chose this specific program.
  6. Submit applications — Apply through the official university portal or scholarship portal (e.g., OASIS for Australia Awards). Double-check all uploaded documents.
  7. Track deadlines — Maintain a spreadsheet with deadlines, submission status, and follow-up dates for each application.
  8. Prepare for interviews — Some scholarships conduct interviews. Practice common questions about your research, career goals, and reasons for choosing Australia.
  9. Apply for visa — After receiving an offer letter and Confirmation of Enrollment (CoE), apply for a Student Visa (subclass 500).
  10. Accept and enroll — Accept your scholarship offer, enroll in courses, book flights, arrange accommodation, and prepare for your journey to Australia.

SOP Writing Tips & Email Template for Professors — Australia Scholarships 2026–2027

How to Write a Winning Statement of Purpose for Australian Scholarships

Your Statement of Purpose (SOP) is often the deciding factor in scholarship decisions. Follow these tips specifically for Australian scholarships 2026–2027:

  • Start with your motivation — Explain why you want to study in Australia specifically, not just any country. Mention specific Australian universities, research centers, or professors.
  • Connect your past to your future — Show how your previous education and experience have prepared you for this program and how the scholarship will help you achieve your career goals.
  • Address the selection criteria — Australia Awards and other government scholarships prioritize candidates who will contribute to their home country's development. Explicitly address how your studies will benefit your community.
  • Be specific about the program — Name courses, research groups, labs, or faculty members you want to work with. Generic SOPs are easily rejected.
  • Keep it concise — 800–1,200 words is ideal. Use clear paragraphs and avoid repetition. Full SOP writing guide →

Common Mistakes to Avoid for 2026–2027 Applicants

  • Missing deadlines — Australian scholarships have strict deadlines. Late submissions are rarely accepted. Put every deadline in your calendar with reminders 2 weeks and 1 week before.
  • Wrong documents — Submitting untranslated transcripts, unverified documents, or missing pages can disqualify your application.
  • Expired links — Scholarship links change annually. Always verify you are on the official current page for the 2026–2027 cycle.
  • Fake scholarships — Never pay for scholarship applications. Legitimate Australian scholarships never charge application fees. Report scams to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission.
  • Poor SOP — Generic statements like "I want to study in Australia because it has good universities" hurt your application. Be specific about faculty, research, and career plans.
  • Ignoring eligibility — Applying for scholarships you clearly do not qualify for wastes time. Filter by nationality, degree level, and academic requirements before applying.
  • Not contacting supervisors — For PhD and research master's applications, not reaching out to potential supervisors beforehand significantly reduces your chances.
  • Weak MOI letter — An MOI letter that does not explicitly state "English was the sole medium of instruction" may be rejected. Have your university use the exact phrasing required.

Cost of Studying in Australia Without a Scholarship

If you do not receive a full scholarship, budget approximately:

  • Tuition fees: AUD $20,000–$50,000 per year depending on program and university
  • Living expenses: AUD $21,000–$30,000 per year (Department of Home Affairs minimum: AUD $24,505)
  • Health cover (OSHC): AUD $500–$700 per year for single student
  • Accommodation: AUD $250–$500 per week (varies by city)
  • Total annual cost (indicative): AUD $45,000–$80,000

Part-time work (48 hours/fortnight) at AUD $24–$30/hour can offset significant living costs.

Australian National University, University of Sydney, University of Melbourne, Monash University, University of Queensland, UNSW, University of Adelaide, Bond University, Swinburne, Griffith, Curtin, Flinders, Deakin, Macquarie, and University of South Australia all accept alternatives to IELTS.

Can I get a fully funded scholarship in Australia without IELTS?

Yes. Australia Awards, RTP, University of Melbourne Graduate Scholarships, University of Sydney International Scholarships, ANU PhD Scholarships, and Monash Graduate Scholarships all offer full funding without requiring IELTS (accepting alternatives).

How can I apply for Australia Awards without IELTS?

Apply through the OASIS online portal at dfat.gov.au. Select your country, check if your preferred university accepts MOI or other alternatives, and submit your application before the deadline (typically April 30 for the following year).

Is MOI accepted for Australian student visas?

For visa purposes, the Department of Home Affairs requires IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, or Cambridge English. However, the university or scholarship may accept MOI for admission, and then a visa-compliant test can be taken separately.

Is PTE accepted instead of IELTS in Australia?

Yes. PTE Academic is accepted by all Australian universities and the Department of Home Affairs for visa applications as an equivalent to IELTS.

Which scholarship covers all expenses in Australia?

Australia Awards Scholarships and the Research Training Program (RTP) provide the most comprehensive coverage including full tuition, stipend, airfare, health cover, and establishment allowance.

Which Australian scholarship is easiest to get?

University-specific scholarships like Deakin VCIS, UWA Global Excellence, Macquarie MUIS, and Griffith International Scholarship have broader eligibility criteria and higher acceptance rates than government-funded programs like Australia Awards.

How much CGPA is required for an Australian scholarship?

Most competitive scholarships require a minimum GPA of 5.5–6.0/7.0 (approximately 3.0–3.5/4.0). Some university-specific scholarships accept lower GPAs if other aspects of the application are strong.

How much IELTS score is needed for Australia?

If you choose to take IELTS despite alternatives, most Australian universities require 6.0–7.0 overall band with no band less than 5.5–6.5 depending on the program and university.

Can I apply without any English test?

Yes. If your previous degree was taught in English, obtain an MOI letter from your university. Many Australian institutions accept this as sufficient proof of English proficiency without requiring any test score.

What is the RTP Scholarship in Australia?

The Research Training Program (RTP) is an Australian Government-funded scholarship that covers tuition fees and provides a stipend (AUD $35,000–$40,000/year) for domestic and international research students (Master's by Research and PhD).

Which Australian university gives the highest scholarship?

Australia Awards and RTP are the most generous in terms of total value. Among individual universities, the University of Melbourne and University of Sydney offer the most comprehensive research scholarship packages.

Can I work while studying in Australia?

Yes. Student visa (subclass 500) holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during term and unlimited hours during scheduled university breaks.

Can I bring my family while studying in Australia?

Yes. Master's and PhD students can usually bring dependents (spouse and children) on their student visa. Your partner can work full-time. Undergraduate dependents may require separate visa applications.

Can I study in Australia without TOEFL or IELTS?

Yes. You can use MOI, PTE Academic, Cambridge English, or even a university interview as proof of English proficiency without needing TOEFL or IELTS.

What documents are required for Australia scholarship applications?

Typically: valid passport, academic transcripts, CV, 2–3 recommendation letters, SOP (or research proposal), English proficiency proof (MOI, PTE, TOEFL, etc.), and application form.

What is the deadline for Australia scholarships 2026–2027?

Key deadlines: University of Melbourne (Oct 31, 2026), ANU/Monash (Dec 31, 2026), University of Sydney (Feb 28, 2027), Australia Awards (Apr 30, 2027). Many scholarships have rolling deadlines.

Are there Australia scholarships with monthly stipend?

Yes. The RTP provides AUD $35,000–$40,000/year as a stipend (paid fortnightly or monthly). Australia Awards provides a contribution to living expenses. University research scholarships also include monthly stipends.

Are there Australia scholarships with no application fee?

Yes. Legitimate Australian scholarships never charge application fees. Australia Awards, RTP, and all major university scholarships are free to apply.

Can I get a scholarship in Australia without work experience?

Yes. Many scholarships, especially at the undergraduate and PhD levels, do not require work experience. Academic excellence and research potential are more important.

Is there an age limit for Australian scholarships?

Australia Awards generally require applicants to be under 50 years of age. University-specific scholarships typically have no strict age limit, though some may prioritize early-career candidates.

Can I apply for multiple Australian scholarships?

Yes. You can apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously. However, you cannot hold more than one full scholarship at the same time.

How competitive are Australia Awards Scholarships?

Highly competitive. Acceptance rates vary by country but are typically 5–15%. Strong academic records, clear development goals, and well-written applications significantly improve your chances.

Do I need a supervisor before applying for a PhD in Australia?

Strongly recommended. Most research scholarships and PhD admissions require you to identify and secure a supervisor who agrees to support your project before or during the application process.

What is the student visa process for Australia?

After receiving your offer and Confirmation of Enrollment (CoE), apply online for a Student Visa (subclass 500). Required documents include CoE, passport, health insurance (OSHC), genuine temporary entrant statement, financial evidence, and English proficiency proof.

Can I switch from a student visa to permanent residency?

Yes. Australia's General Skilled Migration (GSM) program and state nomination pathways allow graduates to transition to permanent residency after completing their studies and gaining work experience.

What happens after I get selected for a scholarship?

You will receive an official offer letter. Accept the offer, complete enrollment, apply for a visa, arrange accommodation and flights, and attend orientation at your university. Most scholarships provide pre-departure briefings.

Are there interview tips for Australian scholarship applications?

Research the scholarship objectives, prepare examples showing leadership and impact, practice common questions about your goals and why Australia, dress professionally, and prepare questions to ask the panel.

How do I email a professor for PhD supervision in Australia?

Keep it concise and professional. Introduce yourself, explain your research interest and alignment with their work, attach your CV and research proposal, and express interest in their supervision. Follow up after one week if no response.

Are scholarships in Australia for 2027 open?

Yes. Several scholarships for 2027 intake are already open including University of Sydney (deadline Feb 2027), Australia Awards (Apr 2027), and various rolling RTP scholarships at universities across Australia.

Are scholarships in Australia for 2026 still open?

Some 2026 Semester 2 intakes are still accepting applications with rolling deadlines. 2027 scholarships are now the primary focus, but some programs may have 2026 late intake spots available.

Can I get a scholarship to study in Australia from Pakistan without IELTS?

Yes. Pakistani students can use an MOI letter from HEC-recognized English-medium universities, PTE Academic, or TOEFL to apply for Australian scholarships without IELTS. Australia Awards for Pakistan are a popular option.

Can I get a scholarship to study in Australia from India without IELTS?

Yes. Indian students from English-medium institutions can use MOI letters. PTE and TOEFL are also widely accepted alternatives. Most Australian university scholarships accept these in place of IELTS.

Can I get a scholarship to study in Australia from Nigeria without IELTS?

Yes. Nigerian students can use MOI from Nigerian universities where the medium of instruction is English, or take PTE Academic as an alternative to IELTS for Australian scholarship applications.

Can I get a scholarship to study in Australia from Africa without IELTS?

Yes. Students from African nations can apply for Australia Awards (specifically for developing countries), RTP, and university-specific scholarships using MOI, PTE, or TOEFL alternatives to IELTS.

Are there undergraduate scholarships in Australia without IELTS?

Yes. University of Sydney International Scholarships, Deakin VCIS, UWA Global Excellence, Adelaide Global Excellence, Griffith International, and Curtin Merit Scholarships all offer undergraduate funding and accept alternatives to IELTS.

Are there master's scholarships in Australia without IELTS?

Yes. Australia Awards, RTP, University of Melbourne GRS, University of Sydney IRS, Monash Graduate Scholarship, and many other Australian master's scholarships accept alternative English proficiency proof.

Are there PhD scholarships in Australia without IELTS?

Yes. Most Australian PhD scholarships including RTP, ANU PhD, Melbourne GRS, Sydney IRS, Monash MGS, UQ, and UWA research scholarships accept MOI, PTE, TOEFL, or supervisor interviews instead of IELTS.

Can I get an Australia Awards Scholarship in 2027?

Yes. The Australia Awards 2027 round is open with an April 30, 2027 deadline. Applications are accepted through the OASIS portal. IELTS alternatives including MOI, PTE, and TOEFL are accepted.

What is the Australia Awards Scholarship 2027 deadline?

The Australia Awards Scholarship deadline for the 2027 intake is April 30, 2027. Country-specific deadlines may vary, so check your country page on the official DFAT website.

Is the RTP Scholarship available in 2027?

Yes. The Research Training Program (RTP) continues into 2027 with rolling applications accepted year-round through participating Australian universities. Check individual university portals for their specific 2027 RTP rounds.
