Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, the cookies and similar technologies OpportunityNest (“we”, “us”) uses on www.opportunitynest.org, why we use them, and how you can manage or delete them. This policy is separate from, and should be read alongside, our Privacy Policy, which covers the personal information we process more broadly.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, such as your language, region, or consent choice. Similar technologies, such as localStorage and device identifiers, work in a similar way and are referred to together as “cookies” in this policy.

2. Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Essential for the Website to function and to be secure. They cannot be switched off. These include cookies that remember your consent choice so we do not ask you repeatedly, and security cookies that help block malicious activity.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to count visits and understand how visitors move around the site, so we can improve content and structure. These cookies collect aggregated information and do not directly identify you.
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google AdSense to serve, personalise, and measure advertisements. Google may use cookies such as the DoubleClick cookie to build a profile of your interests and show relevant ads on this and other websites.

3. Consent and How Your Choice Is Applied

When you first visit the Website, a consent banner asks whether you accept non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising). If you accept, we load Google Analytics and allow Google AdSense to set advertising cookies. If you decline, only strictly necessary cookies are set and advertising cookies are not activated.

Your choice is stored in a consent cookie on your device. You can change your choice at any time by clearing cookies in your browser and reloading the page, or by adjusting your browser settings as described below.

4. How to Control or Delete Cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings menu. For guidance, see the help section of your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  • Google Ads Settings: To opt out of personalised advertising served by Google, visit Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt-out tools: You can disable Google Analytics cookies using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the Website functions.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by services we use rather than by us directly. These third parties are responsible for their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics (analytics) — see Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense (advertising) — see Google’s Advertising Technologies.
  • Vercel (hosting and edge caching).
  • Chatling (optional chatbot widget) — used only after you interact with the chatbot.

6. Cookies Set on This Website

The specific cookies we set may change as we improve the site, but typical examples include: a consent-status cookie used to remember your cookie choice, Google Analytics cookies (e.g., _ga, _gid), and advertising cookies set by Google AdSense. These cookies are not used to sell your personal information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent change. Any material changes will be reflected on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about how we use cookies, please contact us through the Contact page or at contact@opportunitynest.org.
