Internship - Global

UNESCO Internship Programme 2027

Paid internship with the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization at its Paris headquarters or field offices worldwide.

Deadline: Rolling / Ongoing | Funding: Paid (monthly stipend) | Level: Graduate / Recent Graduate

Overview

The UNESCO Internship Programme offers a gateway into the United Nations system for students and recent graduates passionate about education, science, culture, and international cooperation. As an intern at UNESCO, you contribute to the organization's mission while building professional skills in a multicultural environment. Placements are available at UNESCO headquarters in Paris and at field offices and institutes around the world, covering programme sectors and corporate services alike.

Benefits

  • Monthly stipend to support living expenses during the internship
  • Hands-on experience in UN programme delivery and policy work
  • Professional mentorship and networking within the UN system
  • Exposure to international conferences, events, and diplomatic processes
  • Credential that strengthens applications for UN careers and graduate studies

Eligibility Criteria

  • Enrolled in a graduate or postgraduate degree programme at the time of application
  • Or graduated within the past 12 months from a graduate or postgraduate program
  • At least 20 years of age at the start of the internship
  • Excellent command of English or French (oral and written)
  • Strong analytical, communication, and computer skills

Required Documents

  • Online application through the UNESCO careers portal
  • Updated CV or resume highlighting relevant experience
  • Motivation letter explaining interest in UNESCO's mandate
  • Proof of enrollment or recent graduation from a recognized institution

Deadline

UNESCO accepts internship applications on a rolling basis. There is no fixed deadline for applications; submissions are reviewed as positions become available. Candidates are encouraged to apply well in advance of their preferred start date.

Application Process

  1. Create a profile on the UNESCO careers portal
  2. Complete the online internship application form
  3. Upload CV, motivation letter, and supporting documents
  4. Submit the application for review by relevant hiring units
  5. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for an interview
  6. Successful applicants receive an internship offer with terms and conditions

Official Link

Apply on the UNESCO Careers Portal ↗

Tips

Highlight any academic or volunteer experience related to UNESCO's areas of work. If you speak both English and French, emphasize this in your application. Research UNESCO's current priorities and programmes before writing your motivation letter to show genuine understanding of the organization's work.

Who Should Apply

Graduate students and recent graduates in education, international relations, sciences, cultural studies, communications, law, human resources, finance, or information technology who want to launch a career in international organizations.

Why This Opportunity Matters

UNESCO sits at the intersection of global education, scientific cooperation, and cultural preservation. An internship here provides unparalleled insight into how multilateral organizations address humanity's most pressing challenges, from climate change through ocean science to heritage protection and access to quality education.

FAQs

Is the UNESCO internship paid?

Yes, UNESCO provides a monthly stipend to help cover living costs during the placement.

Can recent graduates apply?

Yes, graduates who completed their degree within the 12 months prior to the application date are eligible.

How long does the internship last?

Internships typically last between one and six months, depending on the needs of the hiring unit.

Do I need to speak French?

Excellent command of English or French is required. Knowledge of the other language is a significant advantage.
