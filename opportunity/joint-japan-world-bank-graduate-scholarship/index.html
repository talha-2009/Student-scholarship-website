Joint Japan World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program 2027

Fully funded master's degree in development-related fields at leading universities worldwide.

Deadline: March 2027 (estimated) | Funding: Fully Funded | Level: Master's

Overview

The Joint Japan World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program (JJWBGSP) is a flagship capacity-building initiative that enables professionals from developing countries to pursue graduate studies in development-related fields. Funded by the Government of Japan and managed by the World Bank, this scholarship has supported thousands of scholars from developing countries since its inception. Scholars attend leading partner universities in Japan, the US, Europe, Africa, and Asia, and return home to apply their enhanced skills to pressing development challenges.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fee coverage for the entire master's program
  • Monthly living stipend for accommodation, food, and incidental expenses
  • Round-trip air travel from home country to host university
  • Health and accident insurance for the duration of the program
  • Settling-in allowance and thesis preparation grant
  • Access to World Bank professional networks and alumni community

Eligibility Criteria

  • National of a World Bank member developing country
  • Hold a bachelor's degree with strong academic performance
  • Minimum three years of paid development-related work experience since graduation
  • Under 45 years of age at the time of application
  • Offer of admission from a JJWBGSP preferred partner master's program
  • Not a previous JJWBGSP scholarship recipient or World Bank Group staff member

Required Documents

  • Online application form through the JJWBGSP portal
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Proof of admission to a partner program
  • Development impact essay (personal statement)
  • Detailed CV highlighting development-related experience
  • Valid passport copy

Deadline

The application window typically opens in January and closes in March each year. For the 2027 intake, expect opening around January 2027 with a March 2027 deadline. Verify exact dates on the official World Bank JJWBGSP website.

Application Process

  1. Research preferred partner programs and identify suitable master's options
  2. Apply for admission to your chosen partner program directly through the university
  3. Receive an offer of admission from the partner program
  4. Submit the JJWBGSP scholarship application through the official online portal
  5. Upload all required documents including development impact essay
  6. Wait for the selection committee review and results announcement

Tips

Your development impact essay is the most important part of the application. Clearly articulate how your proposed master's program will equip you to solve a specific development problem in your country. Choose a partner program that has a strong reputation in your field of interest.

Who Should Apply

Mid-career professionals from developing countries working in government, international organizations, NGOs, or the private sector in development-related roles such as policy, economics, public health, education, infrastructure, or environmental management.

Why This Opportunity Matters

The Joint Japan World Bank Scholarship creates a direct pipeline from professional experience to advanced academic training to practical development impact. Scholars return to their home countries equipped with world-class training and a network of development professionals, enabling them to drive meaningful change in their communities.

FAQs

Is the scholarship fully funded?

Yes, the scholarship covers full tuition, monthly living stipend, airfare, health insurance, and research costs.

Can I choose any university?

You must apply to one of the JJWBGSP preferred partner master's programs. The list of partner programs is available on the World Bank website.

Is there an age limit?

Applicants must be under 45 years of age at the time of application.

Can I apply more than once?

Previous JJWBGSP scholarship recipients are not eligible. If you have never received the scholarship, you can apply in multiple cycles.
