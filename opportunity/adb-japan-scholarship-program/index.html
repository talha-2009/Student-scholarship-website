Scholarship - Asia Pacific

ADB-Japan Scholarship Program 2027

Fully funded master's degree at top Asian universities for professionals from developing countries.

Deadline: Varies by institution (Sep-Dec 2026) | Funding: Fully Funded | Level: Master's

Overview

The ADB-Japan Scholarship Program (ADB-JSP) provides full financial support for graduate study in development-related fields at 30 participating institutions across Asia-Pacific and beyond. Funded by the Government of Japan and managed by the Asian Development Bank, this scholarship has enabled over 4,000 professionals from developing countries to earn master's degrees since 1988. The program aims to create a pool of highly qualified professionals who can drive sustainable development in their home countries.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fee coverage for the entire master's program
  • Monthly subsistence and housing allowance
  • Round-trip air travel from home country to host institution
  • Health and accident insurance for the program duration
  • Book and educational materials allowance
  • Special research grant for thesis preparation

Eligibility Criteria

  • National of an ADB borrowing member country
  • Hold a bachelor's degree with strong academic standing
  • Minimum two years of full-time professional work experience
  • Under 35 years of age at the time of application
  • Offer of admission from a participating ADB-JSP institution
  • Agreement to return to home country after program completion

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Proof of admission to a participating institution
  • Personal statement on career goals and development interests
  • Valid passport copy
  • Evidence of work experience

Deadline

Deadlines vary by participating institution, typically falling between September and December each year for the following academic year. Check the specific deadline of your chosen institution on the ADB-JSP website.

Application Process

  1. Research participating ADB-JSP institutions and identify suitable master's programs
  2. Apply for admission directly to the chosen institution
  3. Receive an offer of admission from the institution
  4. Submit the ADB-JSP scholarship application through the partner institution
  5. The institution reviews and nominates candidates to ADB
  6. ADB-JSP selection committee makes final award decisions

Tips

Identify participating institutions early and note their individual application deadlines, which may differ from the ADB deadline. Choose a program that directly aligns with a development priority in your home country. Secure strong recommendation letters from supervisors who can speak to your professional abilities and development potential.

Who Should Apply

Mid-career professionals from ADB developing member countries with at least two years of work experience in government, NGOs, international organizations, or private sector roles related to economic development, infrastructure, education, health, or environmental management.

Why This Opportunity Matters

The ADB-JSP creates a direct pathway from professional experience to advanced academic training and back to development impact. Scholars return home equipped with cutting-edge knowledge and a regional network, making them effective contributors to their country's sustainable development goals.

FAQs

Is the ADB-JSP fully funded?

Yes, the scholarship covers full tuition, monthly allowance, airfare, insurance, and research materials.

What is the maximum age for applicants?

Applicants must be under 35 years old at the time of application.

Do I choose the institution?

You must apply to and be accepted by a participating ADB-JSP institution. The list is available on the ADB website.

Is there a bond to return home?

Yes, scholars must agree to return and contribute to their home country's development after graduation.
