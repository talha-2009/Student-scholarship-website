Scholarship - United Kingdom

Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarships 2027

Fully funded UK master's degree studied entirely from your home country through distance learning.

Deadline: November 2026 (estimated) | Funding: Fully Funded | Level: Master's

Overview

The Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarships offer a rare opportunity to earn a prestigious UK master's degree without relocating. Funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office, these scholarships cover full tuition fees for part-time distance learning programs at participating UK universities. They are designed for talented individuals from developing Commonwealth countries who cannot afford to study in the UK but have the drive to create positive change in their communities.

This scholarship route is especially valuable for professionals, teachers, civil servants, and development workers who want to upgrade their qualifications while maintaining their current employment and family responsibilities.

Benefits

  • Full tuition fee coverage for the entire master's program
  • Access to UK university digital resources, libraries, and academic supervision
  • No relocation or living expense costs required
  • Earn a globally recognized UK degree while remaining in your home country
  • Join an international network of Commonwealth alumni

Eligibility Criteria

  • Citizen of an eligible Commonwealth country or refugee status in an eligible country
  • Hold a first degree with at least upper-second-class standing (2:1)
  • Unable to afford studying in the UK without a scholarship
  • Have an offer of admission to an eligible distance learning master's program at a participating UK university
  • Demonstrate how the proposed study will benefit your home country

Required Documents

  • Online application form via the CSC Electronic Application System
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Proof of citizenship or refugee status
  • Personal statement outlining development goals
  • Two academic or professional references
  • Admission offer letter from a participating UK university

Deadline

Applications typically open in August and close in November each year. For the 2027 intake cycle, the deadline is expected around late November 2026. Always verify the exact date on the official Commonwealth Scholarship Commission website.

Application Process

  1. Research participating UK universities and identify eligible distance learning master's programs
  2. Apply for admission to your chosen program directly through the university
  3. Receive an offer of admission from the university
  4. Submit a scholarship application through the CSC Electronic Application System
  5. Provide supporting documents including references and personal statement
  6. Wait for the selection committee to review and announce results

Official Link

Apply on the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission website ↗

Tips

Start early by researching which UK universities offer distance learning programs in your field of interest. Contact program coordinators to understand course structure before applying. Your personal statement should clearly connect the master's program to specific development challenges in your home country.

Who Should Apply

This scholarship is ideal for mid-career professionals, educators, healthcare workers, civil servants, and development practitioners from developing Commonwealth countries who want a UK qualification but cannot relocate for study.

Why This Opportunity Matters

The Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarships represent a cost-effective pathway to international education that does not require uprooting your life. They enable talented individuals to gain advanced knowledge and skills that can be directly applied to local development challenges, creating a multiplier effect for communities across the Commonwealth.

FAQs

Can I work while studying?

Yes, the part-time distance learning format is designed for students who are employed or have other commitments.

Is IELTS required for this scholarship?

Many UK universities accept alternative evidence of English proficiency. Some waive the requirement for applicants from English-medium education backgrounds.

How long is the program?

Most distance learning master's programs take two to three years to complete on a part-time basis.

Can I choose any UK university?

You must choose from UK universities that participate in the Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarship scheme.
