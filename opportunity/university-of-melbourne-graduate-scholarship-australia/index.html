Scholarship - Australia

University of Melbourne Graduate Scholarship

Introduction

The University of Melbourne Graduate Scholarship supports both domestic and international students of high academic merit pursuing a Master's by Research or PhD at one of Australia's top-ranked universities. Like several Australian research scholarships, it is integrated directly into the graduate research admission process rather than requiring a separate scholarship-only application.

About the Opportunity

Students applying for a graduate research degree at the University of Melbourne are considered for this scholarship as part of their standard admission review, with the university assessing academic merit alongside research potential. Because scholarship and admission decisions are typically bundled together, a strong research proposal and academic record serve double duty in both processes.

Benefits

Scholars receive a living allowance stipend at a competitive annual rate, a full tuition fee offset, Overseas Student Health Cover for international students, and a relocation grant.

Who Can Apply?

Domestic and international students of high academic merit admitted to a Master's by Research or PhD at the University of Melbourne can apply.

Fields of Study or Available Areas

The scholarship covers all research fields available at the University of Melbourne at the graduate research level, reflecting the university's broad research strength.

Required Documents

Applicants need academic transcripts, a research proposal, a CV, references, and English proficiency evidence for international applicants.

Application Process

Applicants apply for admission to a graduate research degree at the University of Melbourne and indicate interest in the Melbourne Research Scholarship; no separate application is typically required, since consideration happens within the standard admission process.

Deadline

The current deadline is 31 December 2026; applicants should confirm the exact date for their specific programme, since research degree admission can run on a rolling basis with certain intake-based cutoffs.

Why You Should Apply

For domestic and international students seeking comprehensive funding for graduate research study in Australia, this scholarship offers strong support integrated directly into a single admission process, at one of the country's leading research universities.

For full details, visit the official University of Melbourne scholarships page: https://study.unimelb.edu.au/scholarships
