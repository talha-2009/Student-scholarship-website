About the OECD Internship Programme

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) is an international organisation with 38 member countries that works to build better policies for better lives. The OECD provides a forum where governments can compare policy experiences, seek answers to common challenges, identify good practices, and coordinate domestic and international policies.

The OECD Internship Programme invites students enrolled in full-time degree programmes to contribute to the organisation’s work while gaining practical experience in an international environment. Interns work under the supervision of OECD experts on projects that directly support the organisation’s policy research, data analysis, and publications. The programme is designed to bridge academic knowledge with real-world policy work.

Internships are based primarily at the OECD headquarters in Paris, in the 16th arrondissement near the Bois de Boulogne. The OECD also has centres in Berlin, Mexico City, Tokyo, and Washington DC, though most internship placements are in Paris.

Internship Benefits and Stipend

The OECD provides a monthly stipend to help interns cover living expenses during their placement in Paris. While the stipend is not intended to cover all costs, it contributes significantly to accommodation, meals, and local transportation.

Internship Fields and Areas

The OECD recruits interns across all of its directorates and departments. The following are the main areas where internships are regularly offered.

  • Economics and policy analysis: Macroeconomic forecasting, country surveys, structural policy analysis, and economic modelling.
  • Data and statistics: Statistical analysis, data visualisation, indicator development, and database management.
  • Climate and environment: Environmental policy, climate change economics, green finance, and sustainable development.
  • Education and skills: PISA and other education assessments, skills policy, and lifelong learning research.
  • Tax and fiscal policy: International tax cooperation, tax avoidance research, and fiscal transparency.
  • Trade and agriculture: Trade policy analysis, agricultural market data, and supply chain research.
  • Science, technology and innovation: Digital economy policy, AI governance, and innovation measurement.
  • Governance and public management: Public sector reform, regulatory policy, and anti-corruption research.
  • Communications and digital: Content creation, social media, web analytics, and publication design.
  • Human resources and administration: HR policy, talent management, and administrative support.

How to Apply Step by Step

Step 1: Create an OECD Careers Profile

Visit the OECD careers portal and create an account. Complete your profile with accurate education and work experience. Upload an updated CV and save a draft cover letter that you can customise per application.

Step 2: Search for Internship Vacancies

Browse internship vacancies on the OECD careers portal. Use filters to narrow by directorate, field, or skills required. Read each vacancy notice carefully to understand the specific requirements and responsibilities.

Step 3: Tailor Your Application

Customise your cover letter for each vacancy. Address the specific skills and experience the vacancy asks for. Reference OECD projects or publications that relate to your field and explain how your background aligns with the directorate’s work.

Step 4: Submit Before the Deadline

Each vacancy has a specific closing date. Submit your application early to avoid technical issues. Incomplete applications are not considered.

Step 5: Prepare for Interviews

If shortlisted, you will be invited for an interview, typically via video call. Research the OECD directorate’s current projects, review the vacancy details, and prepare examples of your relevant experience using the STAR method.

Step 6: Accept and Onboard

If selected, you will receive an internship offer. Complete the required onboarding steps, including visa arrangements if needed, and plan your relocation to Paris.

Selection Process

The OECD selects interns through a competitive process managed by each hiring directorate. The process generally includes application screening by HR, a technical review by the hiring team, a competency-based interview typically lasting 30–45 minutes, reference checks, and an internship offer to the successful candidate.

The selection committee evaluates academic excellence, relevance of experience to the vacancy, analytical and communication skills, language proficiency, and motivation for working at the OECD.

Tips for a Strong Application

  • Research the directorate. Each OECD directorate has a specific policy focus. Tailor your application to show you understand their current work and priorities.
  • Demonstrate analytical ability. OECD work is data-driven. Highlight your quantitative, research, and analytical skills with concrete examples.
  • Show language proficiency. Fluency in English is essential; French is a strong advantage. If both are strong, mention it prominently.
  • Be specific about your availability. State clearly when you can start and for how long. Flexible availability increases your chances.
  • Apply to multiple vacancies. The OECD hires interns across many directorates. Applying to several relevant vacancies improves your chances.
  • Network. If possible, connect with current or former OECD interns through professional networks to learn about the application experience.

Questions and Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the OECD internship pay?

Yes. OECD interns receive a monthly stipend of approximately EUR 1,000 to help cover living expenses in Paris. This is one of the more generous stipends among international organisation internships.

How long is an OECD internship?

OECD internships typically last between one and six months, with the possibility of extension up to a maximum of six months. Internships can be full-time or part-time.

What fields does the OECD recruit interns for?

The OECD recruits interns in economics, public policy, data analytics, climate and environment, education, governance, tax policy, trade, science and technology, statistics, communications, and human resources.

Where is the OECD internship located?

The majority of OECD internships are at the headquarters in Paris, France. Some placements may be available at OECD centres in Berlin, Mexico City, Tokyo, and Washington DC.

Can international students apply for the OECD internship?

Yes. The OECD accepts applicants from all OECD member countries and some non-member partner countries. You must be enrolled in a full-time degree programme and have strong proficiency in English or French.

When is the OECD internship deadline 2026?

OECD internships are posted on a rolling basis throughout the year. There is no single deadline. Candidates should monitor the OECD careers portal and apply when relevant vacancies appear.
