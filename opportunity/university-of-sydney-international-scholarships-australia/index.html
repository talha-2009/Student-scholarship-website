University of Sydney International Scholarships

Introduction

The University of Sydney International Scholarships programme covers a range of merit-based awards for high-achieving students entering undergraduate, postgraduate coursework, or research programmes at one of Australia's oldest and most prestigious universities. Rather than a single scholarship, "International Scholarships" is best understood as a portfolio of separate awards with different tiers of funding, so the specific benefits and requirements a student encounters depend heavily on which award within the portfolio they apply for.

About the Opportunity

Students apply for admission to the University of Sydney and separately apply for the relevant scholarship within the portfolio through the university's dedicated scholarships system, since the two processes generally run alongside each other rather than being fully combined. Because each award within the portfolio targets a slightly different student profile, from high-achieving undergraduates to research candidates, applicants benefit from identifying the specific award that fits their situation rather than assuming a single generic application covers everything.

Benefits

Benefits vary by specific award tier, ranging from tuition fee coverage and/or a living allowance through to research scholarships that can include a stipend of around AUD 40,000 per year.

Who Can Apply?

High-achieving domestic and international students admitted to eligible undergraduate, postgraduate coursework, or research programmes at the University of Sydney can apply, with selection based on academic merit.

Fields of Study or Available Areas

The portfolio spans all academic fields available at the University of Sydney, since eligibility depends on the specific award tier and admitted programme rather than a single disciplinary restriction.

Required Documents

Applicants need academic transcripts, a personal statement, a CV, references, and English proficiency evidence for international applicants.

Application Process

Applicants apply for admission to the University of Sydney and separately apply for the relevant scholarship via the university's scholarships portal by the published deadline for that specific award.

Deadline

The current application deadline is 28 February 2027; exact dates can vary by specific award within the portfolio, so applicants should confirm the date for their targeted scholarship.

Why You Should Apply

For high-achieving students targeting the University of Sydney, exploring the full scholarship portfolio rather than assuming a single generic award applies can uncover meaningful funding, from partial tuition support through substantial research stipends.

For full details, visit the official University of Sydney scholarships page: https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships.html
