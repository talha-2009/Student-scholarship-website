Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OpportunityNest ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit www.opportunitynest.org (the "Website"). By using the Website you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate and improve the Website:

  • Information you submit: When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Usage and device information: Like most websites, we automatically collect technical information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (country level), pages visited, and referring URLs. This is collected through Google Analytics and is used to understand how visitors use the site.
  • Cookies: We and our third-party partners use cookies and similar technologies as described below.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to contact form enquiries;
  • Measure and improve Website performance and content;
  • Provide, personalise, and measure advertising where permitted by your consent choices;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Website; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site operation and security.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g., Google Analytics, privacy policy).
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google AdSense and other advertising partners to serve, personalise, and measure ads. Google may use cookies such as the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. To learn more about Google advertising cookies and how to control them, see Google's Advertising Technologies page.

When you first visit the Website, a consent banner asks whether you accept non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored on your device and you may change it at any time by clearing cookies or adjusting your browser settings. You can also opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings.

For a full list of the cookies we use, what they do, and how to manage or delete them, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf or in accordance with their own policies:

  • Vercel hosts the Website.
  • Supabase stores and serves opportunity listings.
  • Formspree delivers contact form messages.
  • Google Analytics measures site usage.
  • Google AdSense serves and measures advertising. Google may use cookies to serve ads, and its use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Chatling provides the optional chatbot widget.

Each provider processes data according to its own privacy policy. We do not sell or share your personal information for money.

5. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your enquiry. Analytics data is retained in accordance with Google Analytics retention settings. Aggregate statistics that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR): You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we do not sell it), and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.
  • Other jurisdictions: Local law may provide additional rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

The Website provides educational information about scholarships and youth programs. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it. Where required, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

8. Third-Party Links

The Website links to external websites (including scholarship providers, universities, and government agencies) that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to review their policies before providing any information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption in transit. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Significant changes will be highlighted on the Website.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through the Contact page, or see our Terms & Conditions and Disclaimer.
